About

Brand thinking, carried all the way to production

I'm a creative director and graphic designer with a track record of driving brand engagement and creative strategy for high-profile clients in entertainment, fashion, retail and tech.

My work covers art direction, campaign development, video editing and integrated media, and I lead cross-functional teams from first concept through final production. In Los Angeles I also organize and promote the Temple Art Market, a monthly handmade art market in Echo Park.
